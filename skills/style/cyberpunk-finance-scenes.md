# Cyberpunk Finance Scene Prompt Styles

Use these scene prompts as composable starters for image/video generation.

## Scene 1 - Hook (Fullbleed)

Prompt:

`cyberpunk financial district, neon market displays, holographic candlestick overlays, epic wide hero framing, high urgency opening beat, neon noir cyberpunk aesthetic, volumetric haze, teal cyan and hot magenta accents, ultra detailed, cinematic color grading`

## Scene 2 - Thesis (50% Bleed)

Prompt:

`cyberpunk analyst character in split-composition framing, side-weighted layout with negative space for headline, risk narrative tension, premium finance editorial look, coherent lighting, high legibility`

## Scene 3 - Data Support (33% Bleed)

Prompt:

`supporting side portrait panel for cyberpunk analyst, chart-friendly atmosphere, reduced background clutter in data zone, neon noir accents, stable contrast, crisp detail`

## Scene 4 - Close (25% Bleed)

Prompt:

`tight inset cyberpunk market moment, edge-anchored composition, confident closing beat, premium commercial style, cinematic reflections, smooth visual coherence`

## Prompt Assembly Pattern

Template:

`{subject}, {shot}, {scene_function}, {style_signature}, {quality}`

Optional negatives:

`blurry, low quality, watermark, text artifacts, muddy contrast`

## Seed Reuse Guidance

- Pick one seed per concept for identity consistency.
- Reuse the same seed when generating aspect variants for full/50/33/25 bleed slots.
- Keep the subject and style blocks unchanged; only adjust shot/composition phrase per slot.
