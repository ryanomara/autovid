# Foodie Magazine Scene Prompt Styles

Use these prompts for editorial culinary visuals that preserve clean typography lanes.

## Scene 1 - Hero (Fullbleed)

Prompt:

`foodie magazine editorial cover featuring artisan ice cream plating, macro texture detail, pastel cream and caramel palette, premium natural light, clean center title-safe zone`

## Scene 2 - Story Panel (50% Bleed)

Prompt:

`split-layout foodie editorial with one side dedicated to visual motion and one side clean for copy, elegant culinary styling, soft shadows, restrained composition clutter`

## Scene 3 - Data Support (33% Bleed)

Prompt:

`food culture backdrop with subtle motion, chart-friendly left zone and supporting visual strip on right, warm editorial tones, crisp separation`

## Scene 4 - Close (25% Bleed)

Prompt:

`tight culinary inset visual anchored to edge, premium magazine finish, gentle movement, clear negative space for summary headline`

## Prompt Assembly Pattern

Template:

`{subject}, {shot}, {scene_function}, {style_signature}, {quality}`

Optional negatives:

`blurry, noisy texture, cluttered table props, text watermark, harsh clipping, oversaturated tones`

## Music Prompt Pattern

For background beds (SongGeneration):

- `instrumental only, lyricless, 90 BPM`
- `instrumental only, lyricless, 120 BPM`

Example:

`light indie-gourmet groove, brushed percussion, warm bass pulses, instrumental only, lyricless, 90 BPM`
